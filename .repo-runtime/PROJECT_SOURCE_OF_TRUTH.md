## Core customer situation
The homeowner is standing in a driveway with a garage door that is stuck, crooked, noisy, broken, off-track or not opening. They may not know which component failed.

The experience must communicate within seconds:
1. You found the right service.
2. The company is local to St. Paul / Minneapolis.
3. Service is available 24/7.
4. The problem feels manageable.
5. Calling is the obvious next action.

## Central brand idea
**FRICTION → CONTROL**

Customer state: stuck / mechanical chaos / disruption.
Brand state: movement / order / precision / calm competence.

Do not explain this idea literally in marketing copy. Express it through composition, motion, typography, mechanical detail and conversion clarity.

## Brand character
Quietly confident, mechanically precise, local, premium, serious, organized, responsive, architectural, strong, human and dependable.

Must not feel like:
- WordPress contractor template
- handyman website
- coupon site
- SaaS dashboard
- generic home-services page
- AI-generated “premium” website
- black-and-gold luxury cliché
- emergency panic marketing
