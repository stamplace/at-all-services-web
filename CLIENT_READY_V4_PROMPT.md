## Creative Direction
The site should feel like a serious local garage door service business, not a design exercise. It should be direct, clear and useful. The user must understand fast what the company does, where it serves and how to contact it.

## Site Strategy
1. Hero with clear service promise and working Call CTA.
2. Quick facts strip with verified details only.
3. Primary service categories.
4. Full service list for completeness.
5. Emergency section.
6. Why choose us using only verifiable claims.
7. How it works.
8. Service area section based on verified cities only.
9. FAQ with helpful customer questions.
10. Request service form that opens WhatsApp with prefilled message.
11. Contact section with phone, email, WhatsApp and hours.

## UX Rules
- Mobile first.
- Call is the primary conversion.
- Request Service is secondary.
- WhatsApp supports the contact flow without overpowering the page.
- The mobile sticky CTA must be useful, compact and non-intrusive.

## Content Rules
