# Benchmark research notes — home-service conversion

Research is used to validate conversion priorities, not to copy visual design.

## Strong recurring patterns
- The first screen must identify the trade/service area and make the next action obvious.
- Phone access should remain visible, especially on mobile.
- Local trust works best when it is concrete: real areas served, real photos, real business details.
- Fast mobile loading and simple intake matter more than decorative animation.
- Reviews/licenses can be strong trust signals only when real; this project intentionally hides them until verified.

## Design implication for At All Services LLC
The website cannot earn a premium impression through badges, gradients or bigger headings. It must earn it through art direction: photography, typography, composition, rhythm, accurate details and quiet conversion hierarchy.
